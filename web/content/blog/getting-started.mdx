## The short version

WebToMarkdown copies the readable part of the page you are on and turns it into clean Markdown. It started from a very practical need: copying documentation without dragging along the sidebar, footer, cookie banners, and other page chrome.

That origin still matters. Documentation pages are one of the best fits for the product. But the job is broader than that. The real job is copying readable web content as Markdown.

## What counts as a good fit

If a page has a clear main content area, WebToMarkdown should be useful there. That includes:

- Documentation pages
- Articles and blog posts
- Release notes and changelogs
- Reference pages and help center entries

The point is not to capture every pixel on the page. The point is to keep the part you came for.

## What it tries to preserve

Readable Markdown is only useful if the structure survives the copy. The extension keeps the parts that usually matter most:

- Headings
- Paragraphs
- Lists
- Links
- Code blocks
- Tables when possible

That makes the result easier to paste into notes, prompts, tickets, internal docs, or your own archive.

## What it does not try to be

There are products that aim to be a research dashboard, a crawler, a sync service, or a full AI workspace. WebToMarkdown is intentionally smaller than that.

It is a local-first utility. Open the page. Copy it. Paste it where you need it. Move on.

## Why that matters

Keeping the scope tight makes the product easier to trust. The core flow works locally in your browser. There is no required account, no upload queue, and no hidden sync layer for normal copy actions.

That is also why the product can stay fast. You do not need a bigger workflow just to get a clean Markdown copy of the page you are already reading.

## The framing going forward

Docs are still central to the story because that is where the product started and where it performs especially well. But the product language should match the actual job: copying readable web pages as Markdown.

That is the line the site, the store screenshots, and the extension copy should all follow.
